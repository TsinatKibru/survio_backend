Features

National Food Fortification
Monitoring Infrastructure

A comprehensive, end-to-end framework built to enforce quality standards and trace fortification premix distribution nationwide.

📋
Dynamic Checklists

Interactive, multi-section compliance forms tailored per industry type. Automatically populates mandatory fields for vitamins and minerals.

📱
Mobile Data Collection

Operator-friendly mobile application allowing field staff to submit production and premix usage indicators directly from factory floors.

📊
Strategic Data Explorer

Custom analytics tools providing deep insights, performance scoring, and comparative trends across all registered production organizations.

🔔

Scheduled notification system warning managers about approaching reporting deadlines and overdue monthly submissions.

How it Works

Securing compliance from
mills to market

  • 1
    Onboarding & Configuration

    Industries are registered under their specific food categories (e.g. Salt, Wheat, CSB+). Account profiles are linked directly to verification managers.

  • 2
    Monthly Assignment

    At the start of every reporting period, correct forms are generated automatically according to industry specialization.

  • 3
    Data Transmission

    Technical staff capture fortification parameters, premix levels, and laboratory values using the web portal or mobile application.

  • 4
    Centralized Validation

    FBIRDC administrators review submissions in the control dashboard, verify compliance percentages, and download structured reports.

On-Site Data Collection

FFIMS Mobile Application

Capture and transmit compliance metrics directly from the factory floor. The FFIMS mobile app is optimized for plant operators, providing reliable, secure field-level input workflows.

  • Offline submissions: Log inspection checklists offline; syncs automatically when reconnected.
  • Built-in camera: Capture and attach direct laboratory scale photos or quality seal verification images.
  • Real-time alerts: Never miss a monthly window with direct operational reminders.
